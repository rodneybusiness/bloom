# Life 101 · Source Map

> Companion reference for the **life-101** course. This is the distilled analysis of the source dataset — a RemNote export titled *"Answering Questions + Life 101"* (76 markdown files, ~1.2MB, ~1,500+ reviewable prompts). The raw export is **not** committed to this repository (see §8, Privacy). This document is the course's map of it.
> Not a lesson — no feedback section. Lessons will cite sections of this map by number.

---

## 1. What the dataset is

One knowledge base with four branches, exported redundantly (the 364KB root file is a verified byte-level flattened duplicate of the per-file exports — zero unique content, confirmed by bidirectional diff):

| Branch | Size | Content |
|---|---|---|
| **Answers for My Kids** (v1) | 15KB | ~36 astronomy cards auto-imported from a highlighted 2-page source |
| **Kids Questions 3 (maybe best?)** | 86KB | The v2 that supersedes v1: ~79 carded concepts (~277 cards) + **109 prose questions across 10 domains that were never converted to cards** |
| **The Foundational 200** | 119KB | ~192 items in 13 roman-numeral sections, cosmos → biology → mind → biases → thinking tools → math → history → civics → economics → technology → ethics → practical skills (~269 prompts) |
| **Life Skills & Adulting** | 119KB + 60 subfiles | 9 domains, ~800–900 realized cards: personal finance (richest), housing, emergency prep, food, healthcare, legal, relationships, productivity, vehicles |

RemNote card syntax used: `Concept↔definition`, `Question→answer`, `descriptor::text with {{cloze}}`, `>>>` multi-line lists.

## 2. The three altitudes and the unifying thesis

The four branches are one project at three altitudes:

- **Kids Questions — how the world works.** Mechanism-first answers to what children actually ask, with honest epistemic labels.
- **Foundational 200 — how to think about the world.** Transferable mental models plus, unusually, *dispositions installed by spaced repetition* (Memento Mori, Agency, Intellectual Humility are cards).
- **Life Skills & Adulting — how to operate in the world.** Decision frameworks, priority orders, scripts, and thresholds; almost entirely defensive (protect the downside).

The thesis that unites them, never stated in any card but structurally undeniable:

> **You are a small, biased, temporary pocket of order in an indifferent, compounding, sometimes adversarially-designed world. The same few mechanisms — energy flow, selection, feedback, compounding, incentives — built the stars, the cell, your brain, your economy, and your habits. Your mind runs those mechanisms fast, not accurately, so it fails in known, nameable ways. The remedy is not willpower but externalized structure: tools, scripts, thresholds, and habits, pre-committed while calm. Understanding the world, thinking clearly about it, and operating competently in it are one discipline — and the point of mastering it is to hand it to your kids.**

The whole base is one long answer to one question: *"What do I tell my kids?"* — where answering honestly requires knowing it yourself.

## 3. The Seven Deep Patterns

Each recurs independently in all three altitudes. These are the course's organizing spine.

**P1 — Entropy: nothing good is self-sustaining.**
Order costs continuous energy. *200:* Second Law → homeostasis → rule of law. *Adulting:* preventive maintenance ladders, friendship "contact every 2–4 weeks," the half-tank rule. *Kids:* conservation and cycles — nothing created or destroyed, everything changes form.

**P2 — Compounding: humans reason linearly in an exponential world.**
*200:* the only concept appearing in five sections (exponential growth, compound interest, Moore's Law, network effects, "Compounding Effect… beyond finance"). *Adulting:* $5,000 at 18% on minimums = 30 years and $11,000 interest; the rate-spread engine (earn 7–10% vs. pay 3–5%) drives car, house, and debt decisions identically. *Kids:* magnitude scales, doubling.
Notable hole: Adulting teaches compounding **only in its negative form** (debt traps) — no rule of 72, no growth example.

**P3 — Mechanism over appearance: the appearance is what needs explaining.**
*Kids:* the signature move — negate the folk belief, supply the mechanism ("The Moon doesn't actually change shape…"; "Stars don't twinkle in space…"), and every biological "why" gets two answers, mechanism *and* evolutionary function, kept distinct. *200:* Map ≠ Territory; memory as reconstruction. *Adulting:* symptom → cause → cost diagnostics (squealing belt = $20 belt before $500 alternator).

**P4 — Your mind is a miscalibrated instrument; name the failure mode to catch it live.**
*200:* Section V is 15 bare bias definitions — a vocabulary to run against your own thinking, with Section VI's tools taught as the patches (Falsifiability patches Confirmation Bias; Hanlon's Razor patches the Fundamental Attribution Error). *Kids:* "the observer is inside the answer" — metal isn't colder, luck is apophenia, you can't tickle yourself because your cerebellum predicts you. *Adulting:* "kitchen sinking," "all-or-nothing thinking," "universal evacuation instinct" — labeled errors you catch mid-commission.

**P5 — Incentives: model what the counterparty is optimizing, then de-bundle.**
*200:* Incentives as the universal solvent; Skin in the Game; Attention Economy (the analysis applied to your own phone). *Adulting:* eye-level shelf +15–20%, dealer four-square, minimum payments "designed to maximize interest while feeling manageable," 85%-of-medical-bills-have-errors; the defense is always the same — separate the bundle and price each part alone. *Kids:* systemic explanation over blame (privilege, wars "rarely a single cause").

**P6 — Pre-commitment: decide while calm, because crisis destroys the capacity it demands.**
The deepest Adulting pattern: the 60% rule (leave at 60% certainty, not 95%), the half-tank rule, HELOC established *before* unemployment, scripts memorized as exact text ("I need to discuss something important. Is now good?" — the only card tagged `##high-frequency` in ~550 lines), thresholds and stopping rules that terminate open-ended deliberation, fallbacks planned three branches deep. *200:* Stoicism's dichotomy of control; habits as the reconciliation of "your judgment is broken" (Section V) with "you are radically free" (Section XII). *Kids:* the safety-question protocols — the one place hedging stops.

**P7 — Trade-offs and scale: nothing is best absolutely; which force wins depends on size.**
*Kids:* gravity wins at galactic scale, dark energy at cosmic; obstetric dilemma; dark vs. light skin as a UV/vitamin-D trade-off; "No system is universally 'best' in all contexts." *200:* Paradox of Choice, diminishing returns, normal distributions — more is not monotonically better. *Adulting:* everything is a graded ladder, never a binary — emergency funds in four tiers, supplies in three, boundaries in three; deductible choice flips at $5,000 expected care.

**The meta-pattern — agency through systems.** The 200 holds an unresolved tension: fifteen cards prove your judgment unreliable; thirteen insist you are free and responsible. The reconciliation, enacted everywhere but never stated: agency is exercised through Section VI's tools and Section XIII's habits, not through raw willpower. Systems and environment beat motivation and intention — in every domain, "I'll remember" and "I'll feel like it" are treated as failure modes.

## 4. Branch profiles — what each is like up close

**Kids (v1 → v3).** v3 earns its "(maybe best?)": it corrects v1's factual errors (27.3-day sidereal vs. 29.5-day synodic lunar period; "violet" for "purple") — though v1 remains in the base, so the two decks now *contradict each other in review*. The answering method is consistent: real vocabulary given as a gift (spaghettification, apophenia, adiabatic), tiered scaffolds (**Follow-up** / **Dig Deeper**), epistemic status labeled (established / "Theory:" / "remains unanswered"), and on values questions the parent hands down the map, never the verdict (meaning of life = five labeled frameworks). Depth follows the kid's persistence — Friction got 18 cards down to "we cannot predict friction coefficients from first principles"; Lightning got one line. The lone directive moment: mental-health questions → "talk to a trusted adult. These conditions are treatable."

**Foundational 200.** ~185 real topics after dedup; the export truncated Section I (a Cosmos chapter with no Big Bang) and cut Section IX mid-item. The proportions reveal the thesis: 59% of the deck is thinking-well + acting-well; 18% is the natural world. Selection filter: does the concept *export* beyond its home domain? Fingerprints: Taleb (3 entries), Harari (Section VIII is the *Sapiens* TOC), Naval's leverage taxonomy, Kiyosaki's asset/liability definitions presented as neutral. Five section-level identities the deck sets up but never states: Natural Selection ≡ Incentives; Homeostasis ≡ Negative Feedback; Entropy ≡ Maintenance; Compound Interest ≡ Habit; genes ≡ writing ≡ money ≡ code (durable encoded information).

**Life Skills & Adulting.** Four knowledge genres: decision frameworks with trigger conditions (~35%), numeric heuristics (~30%), ordered procedures (~20%), diagnostics (~10%). Its master move: memorize the priority order *with its reason* ("401(k) match → HSA → IRA → 401(k) max → taxable" paired with "free money → triple advantage → advantage → flexibility"). The crown jewel is the **LA Evacuation file** — the only first-person, first-principles document in the corpus: named rules (60%, half-tank, wind-check, 405), five scenario routes with failure branches three deep, and the base's single best-designed card, a three-clause direction mnemonic explicitly optimized for recall under adrenaline. The corpus is ~90% "what" cards; that one file is ~90% "why" cards.

## 5. The load-bearing numbers (condensed canon)

The anchors the base treats as retrieval-worthy. (Verify anything consequential before acting — see §6.)

- **Credit:** FICO weights 35/30/15/10/10; utilization <30% mandatory, <10% optimal, per-card AND total; late payments −60 to −150 pts, 7 years on report; 100-pt difference ≈ $40k+ on a 30-yr mortgage; 20% of reports contain errors.
- **Money order:** $1,000 starter fund → kill >15% APR debt → 3–6 month fund (3/6/9–12 by income stability) → 401(k) match → HSA → IRA → 401(k) max → taxable. Emergency fund in HYSA, never invested ("might be down 30% when you need it"), not too accessible.
- **Debt ladder by APR:** payday 400% → cards 18–30% → personal → auto → student → mortgage. Consolidate only if ≥2% lower.
- **Housing:** rent if annual rent < 5% of price; break-even 5–7 years; carrying costs ≈ maintenance 1%/yr + tax 1–2% + insurance 0.5–1%; PMI off at 20/22% equity; repair-vs-replace 50% rule.
- **Vehicles:** depreciation 20/40/60% (lot/yr2/yr5); used sweet spot 2–4 years; 4% rule (finance below, pay cash above); pre-purchase inspection $100–200 always; 90% of drivers are "severe" schedule; timing belt $500–1k prevents $3k+ engine.
- **Food safety:** danger zone 40–140°F, doubling every 20 min; 2 hr max at room temp (1 hr >90°F); ground meat 160°F — **but ground poultry 165°F, the base gets this wrong**; leftovers 165°F.
- **Emergency:** 1 gal water/person/day × 3 days; 60% rule; half-tank rule; 3-2-1 backups (3 copies, 2 media, 1 offsite), restore-test quarterly; fund tiers $1k / 1mo / 3–6mo / 9–12mo.
- **Health:** >$5,000 expected care → low deductible; <$1,000 → HDHP+HSA; preventive must be *coded* preventive; generics save 80–85%; pharmacy prices vary 10×; cash discount 20–50%; first appeal succeeds ~40%.
- **Relationships:** 50/90/200+ hours to casual/friend/close; contact every 2–4 weeks; Gottman 5:1; 20-min conflict break; scripts verbatim ("When X happened…" not "You always…").
- **Habits:** start ≤2 min, +10%/week, never miss twice; environment design over motivation; automatic ≈ 8 weeks.

## 6. Defect ledger — errors spaced repetition would install permanently

Priority-ordered. An SRS makes its contents durable; these are the cards where that's a liability.

**Safety-relevant (fix first)**
1. Ground poultry needs **165°F**, not the 160°F the ground-meats card teaches.
2. The **2-2-2 rule contradicts** the same domain's cooling protocol (2 hr vs. 6 hr total) and storage table (2 days vs. 3–4) — two cards give conflicting answers with no adjudicator.
3. **LA evacuation rationale errors:** I-105 opened October 1993, *before* Northridge (Jan 1994) — it cannot be "built to post-Northridge codes"; the 405/10 interchange did **not** collapse in Northridge (I-10 collapsed at La Cienega/Fairfax; interchange collapses were I-5/SR-14). Routing conclusions may survive; stated reasons don't. No card handles the realistic compound case: earthquake *and* tsunami, where the two routes contradict.

**Legally/financially consequential**
4. Exempt-salary threshold $43,888 — from a DOL rule **vacated in court Nov 2024**; the durable figure reverted to $35,568.
5. Stale-dated tax anchors presented as current: 2024 standard deduction, 2024 jumbo threshold, 2024 IRA limits under a "2026" heading; estate exemption labeled 2025 with the 2024 figure. The HYSA "4–5%" rate silently underpins the entire debt-vs-fund argument.
6. "Universal Tenant Rights (Federal Level)" heading is a category error — habitability and 24–48 hr entry notice are state law; the eviction timeline is essentially California's, presented as generic. The whole base is US-locked and never says so.
7. Reversed card: the Roth IRA phase-out has its answer in the prompt position. Broken-syntax cards: ILIT sequence, oil-check procedure, `{{$}}` orphan clozes, a bolded "cloze" that hides nothing.

**Conceptually wrong**
8. *Foundational 200:* Second Law says entropy **never decreases** (not "always increases"); the General Relativity card actually defines gravity; Diminishing Returns conflates marginal decline with unprofitability; Deontology card contains 16 lines of raw pasted AI scaffolding, never resolved; Occam's Razor definition is a fragment missing the actual principle.
9. Overconfident empirics: marshmallow-test "strongly correlated with future success" (deflated by the 2018 replication); "morning habits 77% vs 42%"; cat purring "known to" heal bones; brain "develops until 25"; Love Languages as fact; the money-multiplier banking story (modern view: lending creates deposits).
10. Interference: nine duplicate prompt-stems with different answers ("Multiple account strategy" twice in five lines); v1 vs. v3 moon-period contradiction; three incompatible HVAC replace triggers; two competing "what do I fund first" orders (emergency-fund phases vs. contribution order) that never reference each other.

## 7. Gap ledger — what the curriculum is missing

**The unfinished 60%.** 109 prose questions in Kids v3 (technology, math, philosophy, growing up, justice, money, media literacy) have zero card syntax — the project's visible stopping point.

**The generative half.** Retirement is author-flagged "(needs work?)" and is worse than flagged: no 401(k) node, zero mentions of Social Security tree-wide, RMD referenced but never defined, no asset allocation / index funds / withdrawal rates. Compounding never taught positively. The base protects the downside everywhere and builds the upside almost nowhere.

**Remediation, everywhere.** A direct consequence of the prevention pattern: no first aid or CPR in Emergency Prep (the 200 has FAST/PASS — both export-mangled); no earthquake first-60-seconds (drop/cover/hold, gas shutoff); **no wildfire/smoke/AQI content despite the LA framing** — the single most glaring hole; no fire response, no shelter-in-place, no food-poisoning response, no breach recovery, no mental-health crisis protocol (no 988).

**Structural absences.** Bayesian updating — the formal engine for the fallibilism the whole 200 argues for — is absent entirely (Base Rate exists, never connected). Cosmos section has no Big Bang (export truncation). Civics is the thinnest load-bearing section: no taxation, elections, courts, constitutions. Kids: no chemistry above the atom, reproduction half-covered, death covered biologically and philosophically but not emotionally (no grief), no "how to say I don't know" card despite it being the method the base models best. Food: no actual cooking technique. Relationships: no repair-when-you-were-wrong script, no endings (breakups, fadeouts), no workplace. Productivity: nothing on procrastination — the field's central problem.

**Abandoned systems.** Tagging: 3 uses across ~550 Adulting lines. Cloze discipline: 1 cloze in 269 Foundational-200 prompts, in a deck that *teaches* spaced repetition and active recall. Taxonomy drift: "Tax Planning" has become a catch-all holding health insurance, auto insurance, and estate planning; estate content is split across two subtrees that never cross-reference.

## 8. State of completion + privacy

The base is roughly **70% built**, and every branch stops the same way: conversion incomplete, systems started and abandoned, one node flagged "needs work." This is not decay — it's a snapshot of an ambitious project mid-construction, and completing it is part of this course (Module 5).

**Privacy:** the raw export is deliberately not in this repository. One file — the LA evacuation plan — aggregates approximate home location, household structure, a named family reunification point, and cash-kept-in-car details. Individually innocuous, aggregated sensitive. Keep the raw export out of any shared or synced location; this course references it only at the level of this document.
